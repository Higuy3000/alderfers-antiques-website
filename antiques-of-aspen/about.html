Where We Are

Four Rooms of the Unexpected

Tucked into the heart of Aspen at 309 E Main Street, our shop unfolds across several rooms — each one a different mood, a different era. Part gallery, part curiosity cabinet, it rewards the patient browser. Wander long enough and the past starts to feel wonderfully present.

How We Source

An Ever-Changing Collection

✦

Our inventory is gathered piece by piece from estate sales and private collections — luxury vintage and designer clothing, Western wear, Native American collectibles, American country antiques, period furniture, jewelry, artwork, and the occasional irresistible oddity. Because the stock turns over constantly, no two visits are ever the same.
